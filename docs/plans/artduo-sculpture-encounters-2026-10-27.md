## Modules and interfaces

- `data/sculptures/selection-v1.json` freezes the eighteen selected identities, Chinese source summaries, original/cast distinctions and curatorial tags; the generated public `sources.json` binds source records and per-asset grants to prepared assets. `visual-evidence-v1.json` records actually visible parts. Unknown creators remain unknown. AI prose cannot replace these facts.
- Acquisition downloads catalog-listed public STL files or imports reviewed external sources bound to their source page, grant and byte fingerprint. The GLB conversion step retains original downloads and its conversion receipt. The preparation script verifies the preparation STL's size/hash, removes invalid/degenerate triangles and negligible disconnected scan debris, welds vertices, normalizes pose/scale and prepares compact/reference Meshopt GLBs. Record actual geometry counts, bounds, cleanup and simplification error. Keep originals outside public directories. Generate a local transparent poster for every work.
- Reuse `SculptureRoom` and its existing photo lighting, stationary background, bounded follow, AO, contact shadows, reader, reduced-motion behavior and explicit phone permission. Add an embedded encounter mode with continue/skip, without the preview's model carousel, room or quality chooser. Renderer and selected model load only near an actual encounter.
- `sculpture-encounter` owns pure planning/validation and a small versioned session record, separate from painting `routeOrder`. Decide once after session hydration. Prefer a chapter boundary after two or more paintings; use a midpoint for a single chapter, never replace the first or last painting. Fewer than four paintings and explicit painting-only/excluded-sculpture requests have no encounter. Preserve decision, selection, anchor and active/completed state on refresh. Persist recent encounters locally when storage is available; storage failure must not block viewing.
- `ExperienceShell` suspends painting observation and keyboard navigation during the interlude, then resumes the original next painting. Painting count, URL position validation, adaptive route membership, detail returns, narrative and keepsake semantics remain unchanged.
- Server-only sculpture media resolution supplies the small catalog and approved room assets to immersive pages and the preview. A separate sculpture publication manifest reuses the existing immutable content-hash prefix, uploader and verification receipts. Extend MIME/path validation narrowly for GLB and posters; keep existing painting release coverage unchanged. Upload only the new public derivatives and any explicitly missing room dependencies through the existing logged-in COS console or explicitly supplied scoped upload grants. No credential discovery/export, bucket policy change, existing object overwrite or unrelated prefix access.
- A bounded, rate-limited sculpture narrative endpoint reuses the configured chat provider and provider budget. Facts come from the catalog; the exhibition query is context only. Cache by facts/recipe/context identity and deduplicate in flight; a rejected/unavailable model response leaves a truthful local viewing note. Do not block rendering or overwrite an open reader when prose arrives.

## Acceptance

1. All eighteen works have verified source records, introductions, rights/provenance, cleaned local GLBs and posters; compact/reference geometry and visual framing are checked. Distinguish scans of casts from scans of original museum sculptures, and distinguish both from the shared digital plaster display material. Missing feet, hands or accessories cannot be supplied by the AI introduction.
2. A session gets at most one 50% opportunity, matching the exhibition where possible. Refresh/back/repeated clicks do not reroll/retrigger or alter the original painting route/count. Recent items are downweighted; explicitly excluded media stay excluded.
3. An actual encounter continues to the next original painting; skip, slow/failed assets, unavailable AI, denied phone sensing, reduced motion and storage failure remain usable. Entering the sculpture does not count as painting viewing time.
4. AI copy is based on the frozen facts and is distinct from the museum summary. Tests cover unsupported factual claims, invalid input, caching and fallback.
5. Uploaded objects pass complete byte hashes, GLB/image MIME, CORS, length and immutable-cache checks before CDN URLs activate. Verify browser fetch/render from the local preview as well as delivery URLs; document any unavailable upload access instead of claiming publication.
6. Relevant UI/web tests, type checking, production build and desktop/phone-sized browser rendering/navigation pass. Physical phone GPU/sensor testing is reported separately if unavailable.
